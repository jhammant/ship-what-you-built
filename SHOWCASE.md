### About the "Shape 3" ones

Two of those don't run on either track, and that's deliberate — they're the
[third shape](guide/02-start-here.md#the-honest-note-about-shape-3): things
that need a process running all the time, which neither track's free tier is
built for. They live on container hosts instead.

They're here because the guide is honest about that shape rather than
pretending everything fits in a bucket, and it's useful to see what it looks
like when the answer is "use something else".
